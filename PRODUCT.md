# Product

## Purpose

Career Agent is a local-first AI Career Agent for Chinese professionals. It adds professional career intelligence to job opportunities; it is not another recruiting marketplace and does not begin with automatic application or broad job crawling.

## Core capabilities

### Career Interview

Go beyond résumé parsing to understand real change motivation, next-stage priorities, deal breakers, evidence-backed strengths, differentiation, career direction, compensation thresholds, risk appetite, and trade-offs.

### Recruiter-grade Job Evaluation

Evaluate an opportunity using a recruiter rubric rather than keyword similarity. The expected dimensions include capability fit, career direction, seniority, compensation, company/business context, motivation, location/hard constraints, risk, and career upside.

### Recommendation Explanation

Explain why an opportunity merits attention, why it may not fit, its largest upside and risk, what has explicit evidence, what remains unknown, and what to confirm with the hiring side.

## Opportunity sources

Over time, opportunities may arrive through manual input, company career pages, ATSs, official APIs, partner sources, and platform MCPs. Every source must become the same internal `Job` before evaluation. V0 tests only manual input and an experimental personal-development Liepin MCP connector; neither defines the core product model.
